Who I am

Turning biological data

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Health Sciences at the University of La Laguna, where I combine wet-lab biology with machine learning to understand how small molecules affect cancer cells at scale.

My work spans deep learning model development, automated microscopy image analysis, and phenotypic profiling — bridging computational approaches with experimental validation in the lab.
