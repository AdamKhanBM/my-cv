Who I Am

Building tools at the intersection of biology and AI

I'm a Ph.D. candidate at the Universidad de La Laguna, working at the interface of computational chemistry, machine learning, and cell biology. My research focuses on developing predictive models that accelerate the identification of therapeutic candidates.

I work with QSAR models, molecular docking, and deep learning pipelines to understand how small molecules interact with biological targets and use high-content imaging to profile phenotypic responses at scale.

I have contributed to research spanning, anticancer compounds and their biological target with collaborators across Europe and Asia.
